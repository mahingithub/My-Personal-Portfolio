Before we start

A few good questions.

Have you built websites for my kind of business?+

Browse the work by industry to see projects close to yours. Every project page shows the brief, how it was built and the final screens. If your industry isn’t listed yet, tell me about it. Most of what makes a site work carries across sectors.

What do you need to get started?+

A short description of your business, your goals, and any websites you like. Existing content, photos and branding help. For video, your footage and preferred format are a good starting point.

How much will my project cost?+

It depends on the scope, number of pages and features. Once we’ve discussed your brief, I’ll send a clear quote so you know exactly what’s included before we begin.

Can we work together remotely?+

Yes. I’m based in Dhaka and work with clients remotely. We agree on how often we check in, and you can review progress and share feedback online.

Can you help with both my website and videos?+

Absolutely. We can plan both as one project, keeping the visual direction and messaging consistent across your website and video content.
